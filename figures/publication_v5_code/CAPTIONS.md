# Figure captions

## Figure 1 | Cohort construction and ancestry landscape

**a**, Construction of the target–indication cohort from genetically supported
pairs through clinical-stage restriction, linkage to parseable GWAS studies,
and complete ancestry trails. **b**, Distribution of consolidated ancestry
records in the supporting GWAS evidence. **c**, Distribution of the number of
represented ancestries per target–indication pair in launched targets (Pool A)
and Phase I–III targets (Pool B).

## Figure 2 | Study volume and evidence stage

**a**, Distribution of supporting study counts by launch pool. **b**, Mean
ancestry breadth across prespecified study-count strata. **c**, Difference in
mean ancestry breadth between launched and Phase I–III pairs within each
study-count stratum. **d**, Discovery- and replication-stage ancestry breadth
across study-count strata.

## Figure 3 | Adjusted and matched ancestry–launch analyses

**a**, Pair-level odds ratios for launch per additional represented ancestry.
M1 is the unadjusted model. M2 adds log-transformed supporting study count. M3
adds the earliest supporting-evidence year. M4 additionally adjusts for the
prespecified MeSH disease-category indicators. **b**, Within-indication
conditional odds ratios. W1 conditions on indication. W2 adds supporting study
count. W3 additionally adjusts for earliest supporting-evidence year.
**c**, Signed within-gene difference in ancestry breadth, defined as the
launched-pair mean minus the Phase I–III-pair mean for each informative gene.
**d**, Marginal approval-probability difference comparing pairs with five
versus one represented ancestries under M1–M4. Points denote estimates and
horizontal intervals denote 95% confidence intervals.

## Figure 4 | Cross-ancestry portability

**a**, Unit-level cross-ancestry portability slopes by launch pool under naive,
FIQT, and Deming estimation. A slope of 1 denotes equal EUR and non-EUR
marginal-effect magnitude. **b**, Difference in median portability between
launched and Phase I–III units, with bootstrap 95% confidence intervals and
exploratory permutation P values. **c**, Median portability and interquartile
range by comparison ancestry and estimator.

## Figure 5 | Cross-ancestry colocalization

**a**, Final ancestry-aware stability classification for every powered
gene–trait unit. Circles denote launched units and squares denote Phase I–III
units. **b**, Range of PP.H4 across the prespecified shared-causal priors; points
show the primary p12 = 10^-5 result and horizontal segments show the full prior
range. **c**, Comparison-level stability classification by gene–trait unit and
comparison ancestry. Robust shared indicates consistently high support for a
shared causal signal; robust distinct indicates consistently high support for
distinct causal signals; prior-sensitive indicates that the conclusion changes
across the prespecified shared-causal priors.
